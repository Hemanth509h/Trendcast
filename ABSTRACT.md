# TrendCast: Time-Series Sales Forecasting System

## Abstract

**Problem Statement:**
Businesses often struggle with unpredictable sales fluctuations, leading to either inventory shortages or excessive overhead. Traditional manual tracking lacks the analytical depth required to identify complex seasonal patterns and trends in historical sales data, making future planning reactive rather than proactive.

**Objective:**
The objective of TrendCast is to provide a robust, automated sales management and forecasting platform. By leveraging the SARIMA (Seasonal AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average) model, the system aims to transform raw historical sales data into actionable future insights, enabling data-driven decision-making for inventory and resource allocation.

**Summary & Expected Outcome:**
TrendCast integrates a React-based interactive frontend with a FastAPI backend to offer a seamless data management experience. Users can upload, edit, and visualize sales records through an intuitive dashboard. The application processes this data to generate 12-month forecasts, accounting for seasonality and trends. The expected outcome is a significant improvement in forecasting accuracy and operational efficiency, providing users with a clear visual roadmap of their projected sales performance.
